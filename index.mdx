APIG is a persistent, structured information system for investigating and documenting government, public officials, agencies, organizations, records, meetings, sources, evidence, relationships, and news. The website is a presentation layer over a structured database where people, positions, agencies, jurisdictions, documents, meetings, and relationships are distinct persistent entities with IDs, provenance, and verification status.

## How APIG Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect">
    Information is collected from public sources and documents, including government records, news reports, FOIA responses, and official filings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Extract and Organize">
    Information is extracted and organized into the structured database, connecting people, positions, agencies, and relationships.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Authorize">
    Humans review and authorize publication. Substantive publication and consequential actions require human review and authorization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Present">
    The structured database powers the public-facing website, presenting verified information with full provenance.
  </Step>
</Steps>
