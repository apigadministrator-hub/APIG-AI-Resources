The Accountability Project (APIG) is a public-interest investigative information system that tracks Illinois government, public officials, agencies, records, and meetings. We build persistent, sourced records so citizens, journalists, and researchers can follow the money, the votes, and the decisions that shape their communities. Coverage begins with Wayne County, Illinois and expands across the state's 102 counties.

## Current Information

Recent additions and developments across the Accountability Project. Updated by hand as research proceeds.

- Wayne County Board, elected officials, Housing Authority, and City of Fairfield have active coverage.
- Placeholder landing pages exist for all 102 Illinois counties.
- Wayne County sub-navigation now includes Courts, State's Attorney, Sheriff's Office, Municipalities, School Districts, Fire Protection Districts, People, Organizations, Documents, and News.

## How to Navigate

Every record follows a geographic path: **State → County → City or Township → Agency → Position → Person**. Start with Illinois, drill into a county, then into its cities and townships, then into the agencies that serve them. Each agency lists its positions and the people who hold them. Every entity has a persistent record with attached sources and a verification status.

## Two identities

APIG and APIG Monster News Media are separate identities. Switch between them using the product switcher at the top of the site.

- **APIG** — the primary investigative information system. This site.
- **APIG Monster News Media** — the distinct news and media identity. See the [News product](/news/overview).
