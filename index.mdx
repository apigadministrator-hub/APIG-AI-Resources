The Accountability Project is a public-interest investigative information system that tracks Illinois government, public officials, agencies, records, meetings, and accountability. We build persistent, sourced records so citizens, journalists, and researchers can follow the money, the votes, and the decisions that shape their communities. Our coverage currently centers on Wayne County, Illinois, with a framework designed to expand across the state.

## Currently Documenting

<Note>
  The Accountability Project begins with Wayne County, Illinois. Our system is built to scale: as we add more counties and municipalities, the same geographic drill-down structure will apply to every new jurisdiction.
</Note>

## How to Navigate

Every record in the Accountability Project follows a geographic path: **State \> County \> City or Township \> Agency \> Position \> Person**. Start with Illinois, drill into a county, then into its cities and townships, then into the agencies that serve them. Each agency lists its positions and the people who hold them. Every entity has a persistent record with attached sources and a verification status, so you can see where the information came from and how current it is.
