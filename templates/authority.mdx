<Note>
  A public housing authority is a government entity. Its actions, finances, policies, and governance are matters of public record. Accountability for the authority's operations travels upward through the board, the appointing authority, the county, and the State of Illinois.
</Note>

The Wayne County Housing Authority is a public housing authority serving residents of Wayne County, Illinois. This page documents its governing structure, leadership, staff, and public records as part of the Accountability Project's government transparency initiative.

## The Complete Accountability Chain

This table shows every level of authority and accountability for the Wayne County Housing Authority, from the frontline worker to the federal government. Each level has responsibilities that cannot be passed off to the level below it.

| Level | Entity | Role | Records |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Frontline Staff | Maintenance, Grounds, Office | Perform operations; source of direct evidence | Timesheets, work orders, incident reports |
| 2. Executive Director | WCHA ED | Administers operations; supervises staff | ED reports, compliance filings, communications |
| 3. Board of Commissioners | WCHA Board | Governs, sets policy, oversees ED | Meeting minutes, budgets, resolutions, audits |
| 4. Appointing Authority | \[Pending\] | Appoints board members | Appointment records, authority law/bylaws |
| 5. Wayne County | County Board/Administration | County relationship (funding, oversight, creation) | County ordinances, intergovernmental agreements |
| 6. State of Illinois | IHDA, DCEO | State oversight, licensing, funding compliance | IHDA audits, inspection reports, state funding records |
| 7. Federal | HUD | Federal housing program compliance | HUD inspection reports, REAC scores, notices of deficiency |

<Note>
  The frontline worker is the starting point of inquiry, not the endpoint. When you ask questions about housing conditions, maintenance delays, or staff conduct, you are asking about a system of accountability that extends through every level above that worker.
</Note>

## What Cannot Be Delegated Away

<Warning>
  Public housing authorities are created by state law and funded with public money. The board cannot delegate governing responsibility to the ED. The appointing authority cannot disclaim responsibility for the board it appointed. The county cannot disclaim responsibility for an authority it created or funds. The State of Illinois cannot disclaim responsibility for authorities it licenses and regulates.
</Warning>

## Questions to Ask About This Authority

<Accordion title="About governance">
  Who created WCHA and under what authority? Who appoints the board? What are the terms and qualifications for board members? When were current members appointed?

<Accordion title="About the ED">

<Accordion title="About finances">

</Accordion>

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="About HUD">

</Accordion>

</Accordion>

## Escalation

| Level | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Frontline concern | Document and report to supervisor or ED |
| ED unresponsive | Contact Board of Commissioners |
| Board unresponsive | Contact appointing authority and request county board review |
| County unresponsive | File complaint with Illinois Housing Development Authority (IHDA) |
| State unresponsive | Contact HUD Office of Inspector General or Illinois Attorney General |
