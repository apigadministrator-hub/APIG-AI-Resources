The Executive Director of the Wayne County Housing Authority sits at the center of the accountability chain. The ED translates board policy into daily operations, supervises every staff member who carries out that work, and reports back to the board on what is happening in the field. This page shows where the ED fits between the frontline workers below and the governing board above, what responsibilities the ED must keep, and how to trace authority upward all the way to the State of Illinois.

## Position in the Accountability Chain

The Executive Director is the hinge between the governing board and the frontline workers. Every order that reaches a maintenance worker, every inspection that is performed, and every record that is kept flows through the ED's office. The ED is where board policy becomes staff action, and where staff problems must be reported back to the board.

| Direction | Level | Entity | Relationship |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Above | Governing Board | WCHA Board of Commissioners | ED reports to and is appointed by the Board |
| Above | Appointing Authority | \[Pending\] | Board was appointed by this authority |
| Above | Wayne County | Wayne County Board | County relationship to WCHA (pending verification) |
| Above | State of Illinois | IHDA, HUD | State and federal oversight of WCHA operations |
| Below | Staff | All WCHA employees | ED directly supervises or delegates supervision |
| Below | Frontline Workers | Maintenance, Grounds, Office | Staff carry out work under ED's administrative authority |

## What the Executive Director Is Responsible For

<Note>
  The Executive Director carries the following responsibilities. These duties are administrative in nature and belong to the ED's office. They cannot be shifted downward to frontline workers without the ED retaining oversight and accountability.

  - Day-to-day operations of the housing authority
  - Supervision of all WCHA staff, directly or through subordinate supervisors
  - Implementing board policy and directives
  - Ensuring compliance with HUD requirements and Illinois housing law
  - Maintaining records, preparing reports, and presenting to the board
  - Executing contracts and managing funds within board-approved budgets
  - Reporting known problems, violations, or safety concerns to the board
</Note>

## What Cannot Be Delegated Away

<Warning>
  The Executive Director cannot assign administrative responsibility for compliance, safety, training, or recordkeeping to a frontline worker and then claim no knowledge of failures. The ED's duty is to know, report, and correct. Failure to know is itself an accountability question.
</Warning>

## Questions to Ask the Executive Director

<Accordion title="About supervision">
  What is the ED's supervisory structure? How many direct reports does the ED have? How often are staff performance reviews conducted? Are supervisors trained and evaluated on their management duties?
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="About policy">
  What written policies govern maintenance, inspections, safety, and recordkeeping? When were these policies last updated? Who approved them? Are staff trained on them?
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="About the board">
  How often does the ED report to the board? What information is included in those reports? What information is withheld? Are problems disclosed promptly or only after external complaints?
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="About compliance">
  Is WCHA current on all HUD reporting requirements? Has WCHA received any HUD notices of deficiency, corrective action, or monitoring reviews in the past five years? How were those resolved?
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="About records">
  What maintenance logs, work orders, incident reports, and inspection records are maintained? Where are they stored? Are they accessible to residents and the public via FOIA? What is the retention schedule?
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="About compensation">
  What is the ED's salary, benefits, and contract terms? Is the contract for a fixed term or at-will? Is compensation set by the board in open session? Is it public record?
</Accordion>

## Escalation

| Path | Action | Contact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internal | File a complaint with the WCHA Board of Commissioners | WCHA Board Secretary |
| FOIA | Request public records from WCHA, IHDA, or HUD | See FOIA officer contacts |
| State | Contact the Illinois Housing Development Authority (IHDA) for state-level oversight | IHDA Public Information |
| Federal | Contact HUD Office of Inspector General (OIG) for fraud, waste, or abuse | HUD OIG Hotline |
| Legal | Contact the Illinois Attorney General for civil rights or consumer protection matters | IL AG Public Access Bureau |
| County | Raise the issue with the Wayne County Board if WCHA has a county relationship | Wayne County Board Clerk |
| Public | Attend and speak at public board meetings | WCHA meeting schedule |
| Media | Contact local or state journalists covering public housing or government accountability | See local newsroom contacts |
