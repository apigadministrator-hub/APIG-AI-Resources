The Executive Director is the chief administrative officer of the Wayne County Housing Authority. Appointed by the Board of Commissioners, the Executive Director manages staff, signs contracts, oversees compliance, and is responsible for the day-to-day operation of a public agency. This profile traces the Executive Director's position in the full accountability chain, from staff supervised to board that appointed them to state and federal oversight above.

## Accountability Chain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Staff Supervised">
    Every maintenance worker, office employee, and contractor who reports to the Executive Director operates under this person's authority. The ED's management decisions — staffing, training, scheduling, discipline, resource allocation — directly determine what gets done, how well, and how safely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="This Executive Director">
    Sets the tone for the authority. Decides what gets reported to the board and what doesn't. Signs contracts. Responds to complaints. Implements policy. Has access to all operational records. Cannot claim ignorance of what was in the records they were responsible for maintaining.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Board of Commissioners">
    The Board hired the Executive Director and is responsible for evaluating, retaining, or removing them. A board that keeps a failing executive director is accountable for the ongoing failure. An executive director who misleads the board remains personally accountable for the deception.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wayne County">
    The county oversees the authority's existence and may have appointed the board that hired the Executive Director. County government is part of the accountability structure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="State of Illinois">
    The ED is responsible for maintaining compliance with Illinois housing law, reporting requirements, and any applicable state program requirements. Non-compliance is documented in state records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="HUD">
    Federal compliance is the ED's operational responsibility. HUD findings, corrective action plans, and monitoring reports directly reflect the Executive Director's administration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The Executive Director cannot delegate personal legal responsibility for compliance. Assigning a task to a subordinate does not transfer the ED's legal obligation to ensure it was done correctly. If the ED had records showing a problem and did not act, the problem reflects on the ED, not only on the worker who created it.
</Warning>

## Questions for the Executive Director

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Compliance Status">
    What is the current compliance status with HUD and state requirements? Are there any open findings or corrective action plans? When was the last monitoring review, and what was the result?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Audit History">
    What do the last three audits show? Were there repeat findings? Were corrective actions implemented on schedule? Did the board receive and discuss each audit?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Complaint Records">
    How many resident complaints were received in the past 12 months? How many were resolved? What is the average response time? Are complaint logs maintained and reviewed?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Staff Training">
    What training is provided for maintenance and office staff? Is safety training documented? Are background checks completed? What is the staff turnover rate?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Contract Management">
    What contracts has the ED signed in the past 24 months? Were they competitively bid? Does the board review and approve contracts? Are contract performance standards monitored?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Board Reporting Practices">
    What does the ED report to the board at each meeting? Are financial reports provided monthly? Are problem areas disclosed proactively or only when asked? Does the board receive complete information or summaries?
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Escalation Paths

| Issue | Contact | Method | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ED misconduct or failure | Board of Commissioners | Public comment at board meeting / written complaint | Request action on record |
| Financial mismanagement | Illinois State Auditor | Hotline / formal complaint | Include documentation |
| HUD program violation | HUD Office of Inspector General | Complaint form | Reference specific program and finding |
| Open records denial | Illinois Attorney General | Public Access Counselor | File under FOIA |
| Unsafe conditions | HUD Fair Housing / Illinois Department of Public Health | Complaint line | Document with photos and dates |
| Whistleblower retaliation | Illinois Department of Labor / HUD | Formal complaint | Preserve evidence of retaliation |
