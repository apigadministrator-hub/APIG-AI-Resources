## Accountability Chain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authority Staff">
    Maintenance, administrative, and program staff carry out day-to-day operations. Their work is supervised by the Executive Director. Public employment records are subject to FOIA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Executive Director">
    Jill Short (UNVERIFIED) manages daily operations, signs contracts, oversees compliance with HUD requirements, and reports to the Board of Commissioners.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Board of Commissioners">
    The governing board sets policy, approves budgets, hires and can remove the Executive Director, and is legally responsible for the authority's compliance with state and federal requirements. Current composition: UNKNOWN — pending verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Appointing Authority">
    Board commissioners are appointed by an authority established under the authority's enabling law or county ordinance. Appointing authority: UNKNOWN — pending verification. The Wayne County Board has appointed commissioners; see Board records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wayne County">
    The county is the parent jurisdiction. Wayne County Board records contain housing authority appointment records. The county relationship to the authority requires verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="State of Illinois">
    The Illinois Housing Development Authority (IHDA) has oversight, audit, and compliance authority over Illinois public housing authorities. State statutes govern how authorities are created and operated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Federal">
    HUD funds and oversees the authority's housing programs. HUD inspection reports (REAC scores), audit findings, and compliance records are public. HUD can impose corrective action and, in extreme cases, take over operations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Cannot Be Delegated Away

<Warning>
  A public housing authority board cannot delegate its oversight responsibilities to the Executive Director. If the board approved a budget that eliminated safety programs, failed to review HUD compliance findings, or did not act on documented complaints, that is a board-level accountability question. The appointing authority that placed non-performing commissioners on the board also bears accountability.
</Warning>
