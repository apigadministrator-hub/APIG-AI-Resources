The Village of Mount Erie is a municipality located in Wayne County, Illinois. Mount Erie Township is one of five townships in Wayne County Board District 2, which also includes Bedford, Elm River, Massillon, and Zif townships. This page documents the village government structure and officeholders as part of the Accountability Project: Illinois Government (APIG) jurisdiction records.
