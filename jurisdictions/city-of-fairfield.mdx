<Steps>
  <Step title="City Employees and Frontline Staff">
    Police officers, firefighters, public works workers, and other city employees carry out daily city functions. Their conduct is supervised by department heads and ultimately by the Mayor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Department Heads">
    Each city department is managed by a department head accountable to the Mayor and City Council for the department's operations, budget, and compliance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mayor">
    Gary Moore (UNVERIFIED) is the chief executive of the City of Fairfield. The Mayor administers city government, signs contracts, and is accountable to the City Council and ultimately to Fairfield voters.
