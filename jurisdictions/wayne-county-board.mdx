## Accountability Chain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Individual Board Members">
    Each member votes on county business, approves budgets, and represents their district. Members are directly elected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Board as a Whole">
    The full Board is the county's governing authority. It approves budgets, sets policy, appoints board members to county authorities, and oversees county administration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Appointed Authorities">
    The Wayne County Board appoints members to county authorities and commissions, including the Wayne County Housing Authority. The Board is accountable for the conduct of entities it creates and appoints.
  </Step>
  <Step title="State of Illinois">
    The Illinois Constitution and Illinois statutes govern county government structure, powers, and responsibilities. The Illinois Comptroller audits county finances. The Illinois Attorney General enforces public records laws.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Cannot Be Delegated Away

<Warning>
  The Wayne County Board cannot delegate its governing responsibility to individual departments, appointed authorities, or executive staff. The Board is accountable for the entities it creates, the budgets it approves, and the appointments it makes. A Board member who votes to approve a budget that enables misconduct, or who fails to act on documented problems in county operations, bears accountability at the governing level.
</Warning>
