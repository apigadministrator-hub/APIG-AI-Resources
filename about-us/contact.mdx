We welcome contact from citizens, journalists, researchers, and public officials. Whether you have a tip, a document, a correction, or a general question, we want to hear from you.

## Tips and Leads

Tips from the public are a valuable source of leads. They help us identify records to request, meetings to attend, and facts to verify. Tips are treated as unverified leads until independently confirmed through our standard research process. Submitting a tip does not guarantee publication or action, but every tip is reviewed.

## Document Submissions

Members of the public can submit public records, meeting minutes, and other documents for inclusion in the APIG system. Documents are reviewed for relevance and authenticity before they are added. We accept:

- Meeting minutes and agendas
- FOIA responses and related correspondence
- Court records and filings
- Public filings and disclosures
- Official reports and communications

## Reporting an Error

If you believe a record in our system contains an error, please let us know. Include the specific record, what you believe is incorrect, and any supporting documentation. We investigate every correction request and, when warranted, issue a transparent correction with the original preserved.
