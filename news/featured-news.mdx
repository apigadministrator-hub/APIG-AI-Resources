The Featured News area is the top-level entry point to APIG Monster News Media. It highlights the most important current story or stories, giving visitors an immediate view of what APIG Monster is covering right now. This area is updated as coverage priorities change.

## Purpose

The featured area serves as the editorial front page of APIG Monster News Media. It is not a permanent archive. It reflects current coverage priorities.

## What Appears Here

The Featured News area may include:

- The most significant current APIG Monster original reporting
- Stories with the highest public-interest relevance at the time of publication
- Coverage that connects to active investigations, recent government actions, or significant public records

## Structure

Each featured story links to the full APIG Monster article. Stories are associated with relevant APIG entities where applicable. A featured story about a county board vote would link to the board's organization record, the relevant people, and any associated meeting records.

<Note>
  The Featured News area reflects editorial judgment about current significance. Past featured stories move into the APIG Monster News archive and remain accessible.
</Note>

<Tip>
  To submit a tip or story lead to APIG Monster News Media, use the appropriate intake process when available.
</Tip>
