APIG Monster News Media is a distinct identity within the APIG website. While the primary APIG system is an investigative information and public-record database, APIG Monster News Media provides news-media presentation and editorial coverage. The two identities share a technical platform but remain clearly separate in purpose, branding, and presentation.

## Two Distinct Identities

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="APIG" icon="layers">

  </Card>

  <Card title="APIG Monster News Media" icon="newspaper">

  </Card>

## What APIG Monster News Media Covers

APIG Monster News Media covers three categories of content:

- **Original APIG Monster reporting** organized by date, subject, jurisdiction, topic, person, organization, or event
- **Featured news** highlighting the most significant current story
- **Curated references** to relevant external journalism with summaries explaining why each article matters

## How It Connects to APIG

News articles and external references can be associated with people, agencies, organizations, positions, locations, meetings, documents, events, sources, and investigations in the broader APIG information system. These connections make news content discoverable through the APIG entity navigation.

<Note>
  APIG Monster News Media is not the same as the primary APIG investigative system. The two identities are distinct and should not be presented as interchangeable.
</Note>
