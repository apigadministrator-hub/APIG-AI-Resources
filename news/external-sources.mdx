APIG Monster News Media identifies relevant journalism published by external news organizations and independent publishers. For each referenced article, APIG provides a concise summary explaining the subject and why it is relevant to APIG coverage areas. Readers are then directed to the original publisher for the complete article.

## How External References Work

APIG does not reproduce or rewrite external articles. The reference includes:

- The article title
- The original publisher
- A summary explaining the subject and its relevance to APIG
- A direct link to the original article at the publisher's site

The original publisher remains clearly identified.

## Why APIG References External Journalism

Relevant journalism increases public understanding of government, officials, agencies, and public records. By identifying and contextualizing relevant articles, APIG helps visitors discover important coverage they might not otherwise find. The referral function increases discoverability of legitimate journalism.

## Editorial Independence of External Sources

External news organizations referenced by APIG Monster News Media are independent publishers. They are not APIG subsidiaries, departments, partners, employees, or representatives unless a documented relationship exists. Their editorial decisions are their own.

## Adding New External Sources

The architecture supports adding new legitimate news organizations and independent publishers without redesigning the News Media section. Each external source is treated as a distinct entity with its own record in the APIG information architecture.

<Warning>
  An external article referenced by APIG Monster News Media represents the editorial judgment of the original publisher, not APIG. APIG does not vouch for the accuracy of external reporting beyond identifying it as potentially relevant.
</Warning>
