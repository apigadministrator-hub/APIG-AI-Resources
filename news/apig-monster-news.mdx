APIG Monster News is the archive and presentation of original reporting published by APIG Monster News Media. Articles are organized to be searchable and browsable by multiple dimensions, and each article can be connected to the relevant people, agencies, organizations, meetings, and documents in the broader APIG information system.

## How Articles Are Organized

Articles may be browsed and filtered by:

| Dimension | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Newest first by default |
| Subject | The primary subject matter of the article |
| Jurisdiction | State, county, municipality |
| Topic | Thematic category |
| Person | People mentioned or investigated |
| Organization or Agency | Entities covered in the article |
| Event or Meeting | Specific events or meetings referenced |

The structure expands as the volume of reporting grows.

## Connection to APIG Entities

Each article can be associated with APIG entity records. A story about a county official can link to that person's APIG record, their position, their agency, and any relevant documents or meetings. This allows users to navigate from a news article into the structured APIG information system and vice versa.

## Editorial Independence

APIG Monster News Media maintains editorial independence. News articles are distinct from APIG's structured database records. An article expresses reporting and editorial judgment; a database record reflects verified structured information with provenance.

<Note>
  APIG Monster News articles are journalism. They are not the same as APIG verified database records, though they may reference and link to those records.
</Note>
