## Chain of Command

The following shows the organizational authority structure for this position. This chain reflects governance and reporting relationships, not individual conduct or liability.

<Steps>
  <Step title="HUD (Federal Oversight)">
    The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development provides federal funding, regulatory oversight, and compliance requirements for the housing authority.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wayne County Housing Authority Board (Governing Authority)">
    The board sets policy, approves contracts, hires or appoints the executive director, and holds ultimate governance responsibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Executive Director (This Position)">
    The executive director manages day-to-day operations, supervises staff, and executes contracts subject to board approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staff (Reports to ED)">
    Housing authority staff, including maintenance and office personnel, report to and operate under the direction of the executive director.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  This chain of command reflects formal organizational authority as established by the housing authority's bylaws and HUD regulations. It does not imply individual liability or personal conduct.
</Warning>
