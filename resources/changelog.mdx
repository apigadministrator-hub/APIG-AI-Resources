The changelog records every material change to APIG system resources. It is an operational log, not a development diary. Entries should be specific enough that a future administrator or AI can understand what changed, why it changed, and who authorized it.

## Entry format

Each changelog entry follows this format:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Date | ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD) |
| Asset(s) Affected | The asset IDs or system areas changed |
| Change Description | What changed and why |
| Authorized By | Who approved or performed the change |
| Notes | Additional context |
