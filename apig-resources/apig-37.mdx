APIG-37 establishes the strict separation between two distinct identities operating under the APIG umbrella: APIG, the investigative information system, and APIG Monster News Media, the news publication. These identities are never interchangeable and are governed by separate brand and editorial rules.

## Two Distinct Identities

APIG maintains two separate public identities:

| Identity | Role | Domain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **APIG** | Investigative information system | Database, research, accountability tracking, transparency tools |
| **APIG Monster News Media** | News publication | Original reporting, featured stories, editorial content |

These identities are distinct and must never be presented as interchangeable. A reader should always know which identity is speaking.

## Logo and Brand Asset Rules

Each identity has its own approved logo:

- **APIG logo** (`APIG-ASSET-BRAND-001`): Used on all APIG properties except the news media section
- **APIG Monster News Media logo** (`APIG-ASSET-BRAND-002`): Used only on the news media section

<Warning>
  Never use the APIG investigative logo on news content, or the APIG Monster logo on investigative database pages. The visual identity reinforces the editorial separation.
</Warning>

## APIG Monster News Media Content

APIG Monster News Media produces and publishes its own original content:

- **Featured news area**: A section for highlighted stories selected by APIG Monster editorial staff
- **Original articles**: Content produced by APIG Monster journalists and contributors under the news media identity

APIG Monster operates with editorial independence from the APIG investigative database. The news desk makes its own publication decisions.

## External News Organizations

APIG may reference external independent news organizations. Examples include:

- Village Voice
- Edgar County Watchdogs

These are independent entities. APIG provides a summary and referral link to their articles. APIG does not reproduce external articles in full.

## Editorial Independence

External news organizations are independent. APIG's linking to or referencing their work does not imply:

- Affiliation or partnership
- Ownership or control
- Editorial direction or approval
- Shared staff or resources

The reference is a citation and a service to readers, not an endorsement or organizational merger.

<Tip>
  When referencing external news, use neutral language that describes the source accurately. Avoid language that suggests APIG and the external organization are part of the same entity.
</Tip>

## Navigation and Identity Context

When navigating the News Media section, the APIG Monster News Media identity is active. The APIG investigative identity is not presented as the author of news content.

| Section | Active Identity |
| --- | --- |
| Investigative database, records, accountability tools | APIG |
| News Media, featured stories, original articles | APIG Monster News Media |
| External references and referrals | Attribution to the external source |

<Note>
  A user moving from the investigative database to the news section should experience a clear context shift. The header, logo, and tone reflect the active identity.
</Note>
